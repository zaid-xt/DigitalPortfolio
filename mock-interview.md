## 💭 Reflection

**Situation**:
I participated in a recorded mock interview designed to replicate a real-world job interview environment to assess and improve my professional communication and presentation skills.

**Task**:
The objective was to demonstrate effective interview techniques, confidence, and professional etiquette while responding to structured and situational questions.

**Action**:
I prepared for the session by reviewing common interview questions and practicing answers using the STAR technique. During the interview, I focused on maintaining a calm and confident demeanor, making eye contact, and articulating my responses clearly.

**Result**:
The mock interview experience gave me valuable insight into my strengths and areas for growth. Watching the recording and reviewing feedback helped me improve my delivery, posture, and clarity. This exercise significantly boosted my confidence and readiness for real-world interviews.
