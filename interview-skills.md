## 💬 Reflection

Situation:
I wanted to enhance my readiness for job interviews and improve my ability to present myself confidently to potential employers.

Task:
The goal was to develop strong interview skills, including answering behavioral questions effectively, managing nerves, and demonstrating professionalism.

Action:
I completed both the LinkedIn Learning course and the in-class interview course. I practiced answering questions using the STAR technique, conducted mock interviews, and applied feedback from instructors and peers to refine my responses and body language.

Result:
I gained practical skills in structuring responses, managing professional presentation, and communicating confidently during interviews. Completing both courses and practicing mock interviews significantly increased my preparedness for real-world job applications.
